# Maritime Ferry & Weather Dashboard

## P — Problem & constraints

Combine ferry schedules and maritime-condition data in a usable no-code interface despite external data-format and presentation constraints.

## A — Architecture & decisions

External REST/JSON sources feed Bubble.io data/workflows. Data is normalized for filtering/search and custom HTML/CSS is used for presentation where standard components are insufficient.

## C — Contribution & delivery

Configured data integrations, weather/sea-state presentation, ferry scheduling views, filtering-oriented data structures and custom responsive visual blocks.

## E — Evidence & outcomes

The private evidence archive contains the original project brief and a delivery/demo video. Public claims are limited to the documented implemented capabilities and do not invent quantitative outcomes.

## Confidentiality

Workspace access, provider secrets, client identity, private media and commercial details remain private.
